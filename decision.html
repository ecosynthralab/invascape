Decision Layer

Priority tiers & remediation guidance, joined by Plot ID
This layer combines habitat condition, field-recorded infestation, and your GEE predictors (dist_road, dist_cropland) into a priority tier per plot. These predictors are used here as observed habitat-association flags — the strongest and most coherent variables in your SDM — not as predicted invasion probabilities, since current SDM performance is near-chance at this sample size. Treat tiers as decision support, not automated determinations.
Spatial Predictors
dist_road and dist_cropland values are matched automatically to each plot's GPS point against a predictor grid exported once from GEE (see PREDICTOR_GRID_SETUP.md). No manual import needed for normal use.
